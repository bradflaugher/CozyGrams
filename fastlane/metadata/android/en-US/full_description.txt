A cozy evening, a picture made of clues, and a second controller if someone sits down beside you.

CozyGrams is an endless nonogram for Android TV. Play solo, or hand someone a second controller and solve one shared picture together. Each controller keeps its own player, cursor, and color. The first controller to send input becomes Rose, and the second becomes Sky. They share the credit, and there is no scoreboard. The Story Book turns each finished puzzle into a little keepsake and remembers the chapters you have completed.

TWO ON THE COUCH
• Rose and Sky keep those identities for the session, even if a controller falls asleep and wakes with a new device id.
• On a gamepad, move with the D-pad or the left stick, and hold to keep travelling. The cursor wraps around the board. A fills a square, B or X crosses one out, and Y reveals one square. Menu or Start opens the cozy corner. Back steps out.
• A bare TV remote has no face buttons, so OK cycles fill, then cross, then clear, and holding OK reveals one square. Menu opens the cozy corner. The on-screen legend changes to match whatever is actually in the room.
• The interface is landscape, made for a television. No touchscreen is required, and nothing is drawn below the safe area.

ALWAYS ONE ANSWER
• Every board is uniquely line-solvable. A constraint solver checks each puzzle, so the clues alone are always enough. You never have to guess, and there is never a second valid answer.
• All 4,608 boards the endless deck can deal, and all 24 Story Book chapters, are checked on every test run.
• The deck is deterministic: 24 named subjects (hearts, sleepy cats, cocoa, moons, rain on the window, teapots, mittens, sleeping foxes, pie, and more), each drawn in 12 silhouette-changing looks at every supported size. Finishing a picture advances the seeded deck, and the title names the subject family.
• Bigger boards are bigger puzzles. Subjects are drawn with holes rather than filled in, so a 20x20 line averages two clue groups instead of one long run. No row or column is ever completely blank, and the first sweep of the clues gives away 59% of the grid instead of 69%.

STORY BOOK
• Twenty-four handcrafted chapters, on a size ladder: four 5x5 evenings, three 7x7, six 10x10, four 12x12, five 15x15, and two 20x20 to finish on.
• Every chapter is its own picture, rather than one the endless deck also deals. Each carries a line of its own, and each asks at least one question the first sweep of the clues cannot answer.

THE ROOM
• Illustrated living-room and moonlit-garden scenes stay visible while you play.
• The score is synthesized as you play: a 3.5-minute evolving music box in F pentatonic, five layers that fade between sections, ten synthesized sound effects, and a 12-voice mixer so both players are always heard.

COMFORT
• Larger text, extra contrast, color-blind-friendly player identity, bolder cursors, calmer animation, gentle mistake checking, and put everything back.
• Progress is a versioned save with a fingerprint of the hidden picture. A stale or mismatched save deals a fresh board instead of corrupting one.

NO NONSENSE
CozyGrams is free. No ads. No in-app purchases. No network.
