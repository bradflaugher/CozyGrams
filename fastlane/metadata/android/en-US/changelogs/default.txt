Plays properly on phones, tablets, foldables and Chromebooks as well as Android TV. A phone or tablet starts as one player; turn on Two players in the cozy corner and a paired controller joins as Sky. A FILL | CROSS pen decides what touching the board does, and HOME and BACK sit on screen. Touch controls sized for thumbs, room for camera cutouts and system bars, keyboard and mouse support, and Back that steps through the game. Volume keys work everywhere, and the screen can sleep when idle.
