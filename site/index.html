Tonight's story

First Heart

Each number is a run of filled squares in its row or column, in order. Fill the runs and a little picture appears.

Tap a square to fill it, tap again to cross it out. Arrow keys and Enter work too.

Made for a cozy evening together

A warm, television-first game with true local co-op, a Story Book of keepsakes, and an endless deck of pictures for the nights after.

  • RS

    Two on the couch

    Each controller keeps its own player, cursor and colour. Rose and Sky solve one shared picture, share the credit, and never keep score.

  • Always one answer

    A real constraint solver checks every board, so the clues alone are always enough. No guessing, and never a second answer to feel cheated by.

  • A Story Book

    24 handcrafted chapters on a gentle size ladder, from four 5×5 evenings to two 20×20 nights. Each one becomes a keepsake page.

  • TV first, phone too

    A ten-foot interface for gamepads and bare remotes, and the same game on a phone as a one-thumb puzzle with a fill-or-cross pen.

  • An endless music box

    24 subjects — sleepy cats, cocoa, rain on the window, teapots, foxes, pie — in 12 looks each, under a synthesized score that evolves as you play.

  • Comfort for everyone

    Larger text, extra contrast, colour-blind-friendly players, bolder cursors, calmer motion and gentle mistake checking. TalkBack reads every square.
