Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-12

CozyGrams (package com.cozygrams.tv) is a nonogram puzzle game for Android TV, phones, and tablets. It has no network permission and collects and shares nothing. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics, and it never asks you for a rating.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, Story Book, settings, and whether you've taken the tour and seen each tip are saved in app-private storage on your TV, phone, or tablet.

That save is the one thing the game lets Android back up. If backup is turned on for your device, Android includes it in your Google account's backup and in a device-to-device transfer, so your Story Book can follow you to a new TV or phone. That copy is kept by Google under your account and Google's own privacy policy; the developer can't see it, and the game itself sends nothing.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the save from the device. A backed-up copy stays in your Google account until you delete it there or turn backup off, and Android may restore it if you install CozyGrams again.

Links out

In the Cozy Corner, Share CozyGrams hands the game's Google Play link to your device's share sheet, Send feedback opens the GitHub new-issue page in your browser, and Rate on Google Play opens the game's page in the Play Store app, or in your browser. Where no app can open them, as on many TVs, the game shows the address instead or leaves the option out. Those are other apps, and their own privacy policies apply from there; the game itself sends nothing.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions.
